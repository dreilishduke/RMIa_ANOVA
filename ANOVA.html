Does where you walk change how you feel?

The study. Trammell, Harriger & Krumrei-Mancuso (2024) randomly assigned 188 undergraduates to one 20-minute walk: a nature trail, a busy four-lane road, or a treadmill in a bare lab room. Afterwards, everyone rated how restorative the walk felt (higher = more restorative) and did some memory and attention tasks.
